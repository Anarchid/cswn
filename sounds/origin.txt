Provenance and attribution for the sounds in this directory.

The 28 Liero-derived .wav files (alive, bazooka, begin, blaster, boing, bump,
burner, death*, dirt, dropshel, exp*, hurt*, larpa, movedown, moveup, reloaded,
rifle, select, shot, shotgun, throw) come from csliero's sounds.snd and carry
the same provenance as the rest of the inherited layer — see README.md.

The .ogg files were added 2026-08-05 for the weapon trigger hooks (dryfire,
reload, empty-magazine and selection sounds). All are cut from freesound.org
originals under CC0 or CC-BY 4.0 — licences that permit commercial
redistribution, as this package's GPL v2 requires. tools/cut-new-sounds.sh is
the exact record of how each was trimmed, levelled and encoded.

DELIBERATELY NOT SHIPPED: freesound 515194 ("rifle, assault rifle mp5, empty
chamber, dry fire" by JoseIgnacioTriay) arrived in the same batch and is a good
sound, but it is CC BY-NC 4.0. A NonCommercial term cannot be honoured by a
GPL v2 package — the GPL grants commercial use unconditionally — so it is
absent from this directory rather than merely unreferenced. The rifle dryfire
above is cut from jackyyang09's CC-BY sound instead. Do not re-add 515194.

Also unused: freesound 818509 ("night vision activate" by hotpin7, CC BY 4.0),
kept out of the package because every file in sounds/ is downloaded by every
client whether or not a def references it.
